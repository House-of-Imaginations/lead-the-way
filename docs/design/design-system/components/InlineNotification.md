# InlineNotification

Inline message that stays in the page: `highlight` with a `support-info` rule, or `support-success-bg` with a `support-success` rule.

**Provide:** `kind` (`info` or `success`), `title` (the bold lead words), `children` (one sentence), optional `icon`.

This is where the privacy promise lives: "Aggregate only. Priya sees team totals, never your answers." Never use it for marketing copy or more than two sentences.
