Most teams want to use AI well. Few are given the time to learn it, so it happens after hours, alone, or not at all. Lead the Way gives your team one protected hour a week, a shared set of rules, and a real task to try it on.

It starts with you, then the whole team.

You set up the conditions once. Your team learns together instead of alone.

  1. You60 min

    Learn to lead it

    A short manager path on safe use, checking AI output and supporting teammates who feel nervous about it.

  2. Your team30 min

    Agree the rules together

    One conversation produces a one-page Team AI Agreement: tools you use, what never goes in, how outputs get checked.

  3. You5 min

    Protect the hour

    Pick one weekly slot. Lead the Way holds it in every calendar for four weeks and sends the invitations.

  4. EveryoneWeeks 1 to 3

    Learn, then apply it that week

    A short module, then one task each person already does: a client report, a follow-up email, meeting notes.

  5. EveryoneWeek 4

    Show what changed

    A 30-minute show and tell. People share what they tried and how they checked the output, then earn their badge.

You see the team, never the individual.

Participation is opt-in and never used for performance review.

What managers see

Team totals, updated daily. Enough to know the sprint is working.

  • Invitations and joins
  • Team completion, as a total
  • Number of applied projects
  • Team confidence, before and after

Dan keeps his learning his own.

His progress, reflections and course activity stay private. He chooses what goes on his credential.

What managers never see

  • Named completion percentages
  • Time each person spent
  • Quiz scores or private activity
  • Rankings of any kind

Four weeks later, AI is part of how the team works.
